About us

Cricket the way it should be

Swindon Risers came together in 2026 with a simple aim: to build a cricket club where everyone enjoys the game and has a say in how it’s run.

In 2027 we take the field for our first season in the Swindon Midweek League, Division 4, with Croft Sports Club as our home. The phoenix on our crest says it all: a new club, rising together.

What we stand for

01 · ENJOY THE GAME

Have fun on the field

Play hard, but play with a smile. We compete with effort and keep the spirit of the game alive.

02 · OPEN CLUB

Transparency in all things

Finances, decisions, selection and communication stay open and clear to everyone.

03 · EVERY VOICE MATTERS

Speak up

If anything feels uncomfortable, unfair or unclear, raise it. No issue is too small.

01 · FOLLOW THE SEASON

Follow the season

Fixtures and results are published on Play-Cricket, so family, friends and supporters can follow the season even when they cannot be at the ground.

02 · HOW WE PLAY

Midweek league cricket

Midweek matches in Division 4, with Croft Sports Club as our home. We play hard, compete with effort, and keep the spirit of the game alive.

03 · WHO IT IS FOR

Players, families and supporters

The squad is 16 players and growing. We would love to meet new players, and families and supporters are part of the club too — not just spectators on match day.

  • Midweek matches in the Swindon Midweek League, Division 4
  • A home ground at Croft Sports Club, Swindon
  • An open club — finances, decisions and selection talked about openly
  • A say in how the club is run, whatever your role
  • Socials and a family day beyond match days

Beyond match days

Cricket is the reason we meet, but it is not the whole club. We want socials and a family day that bring partners, children and friends into the Risers, so the club feels like a community on and off the field. If you would like to play, help out, or support us, the Join page is the best place to start.
