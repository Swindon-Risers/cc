Who we are

Who Swindon Risers are

Swindon Risers came together in 2026 as a new cricket club, built by players who wanted a club that felt open from day one — where everyone enjoys the game and has a say in how it’s run.

In 2027 we take the field for our first season in the Swindon Midweek League, Division 4, playing at home at Croft Sports Club, Marlborough Lane, Swindon. Our squad is 16 players and growing, and our values are simple: play hard, have fun, speak up and be transparent.

Audience & visibility

Seen on match days and online

Our partners reach the people around the club: a squad of 16 players and growing, their families and friends, and the teams and supporters we meet across the Swindon Midweek League.

Match days are at Croft Sports Club in Swindon, and our fixtures and results are published on Play-Cricket, so the season is followed well beyond the boundary.
